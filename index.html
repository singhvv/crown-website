02 / How it goes

From the first look to the keys back.

Most repairs move through six stages. We tell you where your car sits at every one of them.

  1. 01

    Estimate

    Drive in or send photos from your phone. In most cases we write the estimate the same day and tell you what it will take.

  2. 02

    Claim setup

    Going through insurance? We open the file, send the adjuster what they need and book you in once it clears.

  3. 03

    Teardown

    Damaged panels come off so we can see what the first look missed. Anything new gets photographed and added to the file.

  4. 04

    Body and frame

    The vehicle goes on the frame rack, gets measured against factory numbers and is pulled back into spec. New metal is welded in where it is needed.

  5. 05

    Paint

    Primer, colour and clear in a downdraft booth. We spray a test card first and blend into the surrounding panels so the repair does not show.

  6. 06

    Quality check and pickup

    Reassembled, torqued, buffed and washed. One of us walks the whole car before we call you.
